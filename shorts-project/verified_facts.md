# Verified YouTube facts (official Help pages, read 2026-11-10)

Base URL: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/<id>?hl=en

| Item | Verified wording / value | Page id |
|---|---|---|
| Shorts max length | Square or vertical uploads "up to three minutes" (since Oct 15, 2024) are Shorts | 15424877, 10059070 |
| Max resolution | "maximum resolution of 1080p" | 10059070 |
| Content ID over 1 min | Any Short over one minute with an active claim "will be blocked globally"; no change under one minute | 15424877 |
| View counting | Since 3/31/2025 a view = each start or replay, no minimum watch time. Old metric kept as "Engaged views". Aug 2026: first-frame counting on all formats | 10059070, 10008196 |
| What pays | YPP eligibility and Shorts revenue are based on engaged views, not views | 10059070 |
| YPP full tier | 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours/12 mo, or 1,000 subs + 10M qualified Shorts views/90 days. Shorts Feed watch hours do not count toward the 4,000 | 72851 |
| YPP expanded tier | 500 subs + 3 public uploads/90 days + 3,000 watch hours/12 mo or 3M Shorts views/90 days. Fan funding and Shopping only, no ad share | 13429240 |
| Feb 1, 2027 change | Updated YPP terms must be accepted by Jan 31, 2027. To earn Shorts ad and Premium revenue for a month, a channel needs "at least 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days", measured over the 90 days ending the 15th of the preceding month | 72851, 12504220 |
| Shorts revenue model | Pooled Shorts Feed ad revenue; Creator Pool allocated by share of engaged views per country; creators keep 45%. A Short with 1 music track sends half its associated revenue to music licensing, 2 tracks two thirds; no music, all goes to the pool | 12504220 |
| Inauthentic content | Renamed from "repetitious content" July 15, 2025. Not monetizable: content that "looks like it's made with a template", "templated storylines", "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production" | 1311392, 10008196 |
| AI disclosure | Required only for realistic content. "Non-realistic" content, a "fully animated video", and production assistance (script, thumbnail, captions) need no disclosure. Disclosure alone does not limit audience or monetization | 14328491 |
| Custom thumbnails | Available for Shorts, only in YouTube Studio on a computer, verified account; 9:16, 2160x3840 recommended, min height 640 px | 72431 |
| Hashtags | Up to 3 shown by the title; more than 60 and all are ignored | 6390658 |
| Titles / descriptions | 100 / 5,000 characters | 57407 |
| Daily upload limit | A limit exists across desktop, mobile and API; no number published; "may vary by country/region or channel history" | 57407 |
| Related video link | With advanced features, a Short can link to one video, Short or live from the same channel | 57407 |
| Links | Links in Shorts descriptions and comments are not clickable (since Aug 31, 2023) | 10008196 |
| End screens | Page says videos need 25 s minimum; it does not mention Shorts. Not confirmed either way | 6388789 |
| Chapters | Page does not mention Shorts. Not confirmed either way | 9884579 |
| UI overlay zones | NOT VERIFIED. No YouTube Help text gives pixel zones. Google Ads Help 13547298 has a vertical safe-zone image and PNG template but no numbers in text; the image host is blocked here | google-ads 13547298 |
