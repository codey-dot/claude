# Short 01: What if you had infinite strength?

Length 34.0 s · 1080x1920 · 30 fps (animated on twos) · 6 shots · 1 set

## Power Rules Sheet
- **Rules:** your muscles can produce any force and never tire. Your body cannot break.
- **Limits:** you weigh the same, and the world around you is exactly as strong as before.
- **Consequence 1, walking:** anything you push on can only take so much force before it fails. Push harder than the rock can bear and your foot goes through it.
- **Consequence 2, throwing:** Newton's third law. The boulder pushes back on you with the same force you put into it, so unless you are anchored you slide backward.
- **Consequence 3, jumping:** a huge push off the ground sends you very high and very fast. Your body survives the landing (rule 2), but the ground takes all of that energy and craters.
- **Twist / ending:** the power is not the hard part. Control is. A pebble crumbles between two fingers: the hardest skill you would ever learn is being gentle.

## Science check
- Ground failure, the equal-and-opposite reaction, and the landing energy going into the ground are correct physics.
- "Infinite strength" and the unbreakable body are stated as the fictional rules.
- The scale is exaggerated for comedy: the foot punch and the slide distance are not computed.
- There is no medical or fitness advice.
